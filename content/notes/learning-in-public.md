# Learning in Public

A digital garden is a place to collect ideas as they develop,
rather than only publishing finished work.

## Why it matters

Writing about what I learn helps me organise my thinking.
It also creates a record that I can revisit and improve.

## An open question

How can I make it easier to share work before it feels finished?
